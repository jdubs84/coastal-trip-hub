Anastasia Beach Bungalows · The Dolphin

Site 4, Sea Bean Loop (sites 1–6) · Sep 27–29 · 2 nights · pre-arrival from Staylist

  • Address: 300 Anastasia Park Rd, St. Augustine, FL 32080
  • Check-in starts 4:00 PM. Check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available for $35 — call ahead.
  • Arrival: at the park entrance, tell the ranger you have a glamping reservation and show the confirmation email. They give the parking pass and gate access. Follow the map to Sea Bean Loop and take the first right for sites 1–6. Site 4 is the fourth on the right. Parking at the site is limited; large vehicles can use the nearby lot. Max 2 vehicles per site — extras go to overflow parking.
  • Key: the lock-box combination comes by phone call or text before arrival. The lock box is on the metal steps at the front door.
  • Wi-Fi: network Glamping-WiFi, password Ana-Glamping-2026 (not guaranteed).
  • Quiet hours 11 PM–7 AM. Amphitheatre concerts end by 10:30 PM. Fires only in the fire rings or grills. No candles or smoking inside. Buy firewood at the Island Beach Shop — don’t collect wood in the park. Store food securely (raccoons and squirrels). Alcohol only at the campsite, not on the beach or while walking through the park. Loft ladders are steep. No drones. No pets in the bungalows or on the beach.
  • Camp: laundry nearby. Bathhouses close briefly each day for cleaning. Trash is picked up daily at 10 AM; extra trash goes to the park compactor (marked on the map inside the bungalow).
  • Before checkout: take all trash to the park compactor and set the thermostat to 72°F cool.
  • Island Beach Shop and Grill: breakfast, lunch, ice cream, beer, ice, batteries, flashlights, snacks, and beach gear. Reserve kayaks, paddleboards, bikes, and beach gear ahead at AnastasiaStatePark.com.
  • Bungalow supervisor (after hours or late arrival): 904-988-6869. Emergencies: call 911 and say Sea Bean Camping loop, sites 1–6.

Things to do at AnastasiaPaddle, sail, bikes, beach · Sep 27–29
Book online 904-460-9111

Sunday rentals: book online by ~4 PM Sat Sep 26

Online booking closes 24 hours ahead. Same-day rentals are first come, first served. The renter must be 18 or older and holds a driver’s license as the deposit. Life jackets are included. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead. No guided tours or ranger programs are scheduled Sep 27 to 29. Prices from the concessionaire’s FareHarbor pages, checked Sep 25, 2026.

Suggested plan

  • Sunday after the 4 PM check-in: take a 1-hour canoe for all 3 ($50), then walk the beach or the Marsh Trail at sunset.
  • Monday morning: sail the Hobie during the high-tide window if someone knows how, or do a 2 to 3 hour paddle on Salt Run.
  • Monday midday: book a Beach Setup for the day ($60) plus 1 extra chair, and have lunch at the Grill.
  • Monday around 2 PM: ride e-bikes on the beach at low tide ($55 for 2 hours). Bikes have to be rented by 3:30 PM.
  • Tuesday before the 11 AM checkout: walk the Ancient Dunes Trail and see the quarries, or take a quick 9:30 AM paddle.

Paddling on Salt Run

Launch behind Anastasia Watersports. No experience needed. Prices are for 1 hour, 2 hours, and 3 hours.

Hours

  • Park: 8 AM to sundown. Sunset is about 7:15 PM.
  • Anastasia Watersports: 9 AM to 4 PM, with rentals back by 5 PM.
  • Island Beach Shop: 9 AM to 5 PM. Rentals go out until 3:30 PM and are due back by 4:30 PM.
  • Island Grill: 10 AM to 4:30 PM. Fish tacos, wraps, baskets, ice cream, beer, wine, and frozen drinks.

Free

  • Ancient Dunes Nature Trail, a shaded loop with some hills and stairs.
  • Marsh Trail along Salt Run.
  • 4 miles of beach, with the swim area behind the Beach Shop.
  • The Spanish coquina quarries near the entrance (stone for the Castillo de San Marcos).
  • Fishing on the east bank of Salt Run (trout, redfish, flounder). There’s a cleaning station, and frozen bait is sold at Watersports. A Florida saltwater license may be required. A Florida resident fishing from shore can get a free shoreline license.

Cellular & Starlink gaps

Shown on the map (toggles: Cellular gaps / Starlink limits). Estimates for planning — not a carrier guarantee.

Cellular — expect trouble

  • No/unreliable: Commander’s Island boat run + on-island; Ocracoke/south Hatteras if you day-trip
  • Limited: Edisto park side, rural SC to Georgetown docks, Hammocks ferry side, Pea Island, Buxton→Frisco
  • Do before weak signal: download Apple/Google offline maps, save Pack hub to Files, screenshot boat host contact

Starlink — CONUS OK, sky is the limit

  • No true “no Starlink” footprint on this East-Coast route
  • Limited: tree canopy / cabin roofs at every SP stop; bouncing skiff (need clear sky + stable mount)
  • If you bring a dish: set up in a clearing with southern sky; don’t count on it during the boat hop
